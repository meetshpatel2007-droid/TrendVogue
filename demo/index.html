Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, delivery matrices, and return policies

How does the Deterministic Delivery Pipeline calculate ETA?
Our logistics engine queries live Indian postal routing matrices. Metro PIN codes (such as Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru) receive priority 2-day air dispatch via BlueDart Air, while Tier-2 regional hubs automatically route via 4-day express surface transit with milestone GPS scanning.
What is the 30-Day Easy Return & Refund policy?
We offer a 100% risk-free 30-day return window on all unworn items with original tags attached. Doorstep courier pickup is scheduled within 24 hours with immediate refund processing.
Are all fabrics certified 100% organic and sustainable?
Yes. Every piece is crafted with GOTS-certified 100% organic cotton, ethically harvested grade-6A mulberry silk, and recycled natural horn buttons. We strictly reject fast-fashion petrochemical synthetics.
Can I pay using Cash on Delivery (COD) or UPI?
Yes! We accept Instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), All Major Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) with zero additional fee.
How do I determine the perfect fit and size?
Every garment page provides an interactive Size Guide with exact chest, waist, and length measurements in both inches and centimeters, along with model height references.
